🐡 Charge Burst: Hold to annoy a pufferfish until it puffs up and blows its spines off.

Hold to annoy a pufferfish. It gulps, swells and turns red. Let go early and it deflates with a raspberry; hold for two seconds and it blows its spines off. A tiny stress-relief toy, no sign-up.

How to play

  1. Press and hold anywhere. The sleepy pufferfish wakes up and starts gulping water: five gulps, each one rounder, redder and angrier, with its spines bristling and an anger mark on its head.
  2. Let go to release it. Before half charge it deflates like a let-go balloon, zips around the screen blowing a raspberry and comes back unimpressed. After half charge it spits everything out in a burst of bubbles. Hold the full two seconds and it blows up: every spine flies off and sticks in the edges of the screen, and the bald fish grows them back one by one.
  3. Tap quickly instead of holding. Taps on the fish poke it (it squashes and glares at you); taps elsewhere make bubble bursts. Each fast tap plays a higher note, and every 8th tap in a row sets off a chain blast.

FAQ

Is Charge Burst free?

Yes. It is free, with no sign-up, no download and no ads. It runs in any modern browser on phones, tablets and computers.

Does it have sound and vibration?

Yes. Every sound is synthesised live in the browser with the Web Audio API, so there are no audio files to load. On Android phones it also vibrates at the big moments; iPhone browsers do not support web vibration.

Which languages does it support?

English, 简体中文 (Simplified Chinese), Español, Français and 日本語 (Japanese). The language is picked automatically from your browser settings; any other language falls back to English.

Who made it?

Integ Life (integ.life). Charge Burst is one of nine small stress-relief toys, together with Void Harvest, Glass Smash, Chain Pop, Knock on Wood, Lap Cat, Squishy Bun, Scratch That, Peel Off. More games are at games.integ.life.

No account and no personal data. At most, anonymous usage statistics (page views and play counts) are collected.
